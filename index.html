INVESTMENT OVERVIEW

Hodges Ward Elliott (“HWE”) is pleased to present the opportunity to acquire the 284-key, all-suite The Chifley Houston, Tapestry Collection (“Hotel” or “Property”), situated in the heart of Uptown Houston, one of the premier business districts in the country featuring the headquarters of Westlake Corporation, Stewart Information Services, and Landry’s. Houston’s renowned Uptown/Galleria district is home to over 28 million square feet of office space in total, ranking it among the largest business districts in the country, as well as the largest shopping mall in Texas, which draws over 30 million visitors each year. Following a complete gut renovation in 2022 and an up-branded relaunch as part of Hilton’s Tapestry Collection in 2023, The Chifley offers an upscale, freshly renovated, all-suite product. Supported by an ideal mix of corporate, group, and leisure demand, the Hotel offers a compelling opportunity to acquire a turn-key, ramping asset in one of Houston’s most resilient markets.

EXCEPTIONAL LOCATION

The Hotel is positioned along the I-610 West Loop just off Westheimer Road, within walking distance of The Galleria and the luxury boutiques of River Oaks District. Less than one mile away, the Post Oak Boulevard office corridor is home to the global headquarters of Westlake Corporation and Stewart Information Services, along with Williams’ largest office outside of Tulsa. Landry’s and Burns & McDonnell are even closer, with offices on the same stretch of the West Loop as the Hotel. The West Loop also provides direct access to I-10, I-69, and Greenway Plaza, home to Occidental Petroleum’s headquarters. Both of Houston’s major airports are easily accessible. Together, these attributes make The Chifley’s upscale, all-suite product a natural fit for weekday business travelers and weekend leisure guests alike.

COMPREHENSIVE RENOVATION & TAPESTRY REBRAND

In 2022, the Hotel completed an approximately $25 million gut renovation that transformed the former Sheraton Suites Houston from top to bottom. The project fully renovated all 284 suites, the public spaces, and roughly 8,000 square feet of meeting space. It also introduced Rouse Craft Cooking, a new destination restaurant and bar. The Property relaunched in early 2023 as The Chifley, the first Tapestry Collection Hotel in Houston, giving guests access to the Hilton Honors loyalty program. With the renovation complete, new ownership can focus on continued ramp-up rather than repositioning.

DEEP CORPORATE DEMAND

Uptown is home to roughly 2,000 companies, and several of the most recognizable are headquartered just minutes from the Hotel. Westlake Corporation (Fortune #388) runs its global headquarters at 2801 Post Oak Boulevard, while Stewart Information Services occupies 156,000 square feet at Four Oaks Place. Landry’s is based at 1510 West Loop South, on the same stretch of highway as the Hotel. Less than one mile away, Williams bought its namesake 64-story tower for $300 million in July 2026 and plans to grow its Houston workforce beyond 700 employees. Woodside Energy runs its Americas operations from 1500 Post Oak Boulevard. Frost Bank’s 55,000-square-foot regional headquarters at BLVD Place adds to the district’s financial presence. Together, these employers drive steady weekday business from executives, consultants, project teams, and visiting clients, a natural fit for The Chifley’s spacious suites.

THE GALLERIA – TEXAS’ PREMIER RETAIL DESTINATION

Just a five-minute walk from the Hotel, The Galleria is the largest shopping mall in Texas. Hines developed the center in 1970, and it has since grown to 2.4 million square feet with roughly 400 stores and restaurants. Neiman Marcus, Saks Fifth Avenue, Nordstrom, and Macy’s anchor the mall today. More than 30 million visitors come through each year, drawn by the shopping, the dining, and a full-size indoor ice rink. Owner Simon Property Group continues to reinvest in the center, most recently committing $9 million to a new Aritzia store. For the Hotel, The Galleria brings steady weekend leisure traffic while giving corporate guests an easy place to grab dinner or entertain clients after hours.

HOUSTON - GLOBAL ECONOMIC EPICENTER

The nation’s fourth largest city and fifth largest metropolitan area, Houston covers nearly 10,000 square miles and is home to roughly 3.5 million jobs. It is a global gateway city, boasting a history of impressive GDP, population, and employment growth. Home to over 3,700 energy-related firms, Houston employs nearly a third of the nation’s jobs in oil and gas extraction, earning its reputation as the “Energy Capital of the World.” In addition, Houston hosts the largest medical center in the world, the largest U.S. export port, and a globally renowned arts hub. The city has also emerged as one of the most popular travel destinations in the U.S. and continues to see increasing visitation year-over-year. The number of visitors has risen sequentially each year with 48.7 million in 2022, 51.4 million in 2023, and 53.9 million in 2024, representing a 10.7% increase from 2022 to 2024.
